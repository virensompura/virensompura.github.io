01Project overview

A wearable assistive device built on an Arduino Mega that detects obstacles and signals their distance to the wearer through vibration.

Objective

Help visually impaired people sense nearby obstacles.

My contribution

  • Created the wearable using an Arduino Mega, ultrasonic sensors, and vibration motors.
  • Implemented obstacle detection with distance-based feedback.
