01Project overview

Driver development and sensor fusion for low-cost robot sensors, bringing an IMU and a time-of-flight range sensor into the ROS 2 ecosystem.

Objective

Improve the accuracy and reliability of robot localization and obstacle detection.

My contribution

  • Developed custom ROS 2 drivers for the Qwiic ICM-20948 IMU and the VL53L0X ToF distance sensor.
  • Engineered a sensor-fusion algorithm to improve localization and obstacle detection.
