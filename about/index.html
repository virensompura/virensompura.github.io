About

Building complete intelligent robotic systems

Who I am

I am a Robotics Engineer interested in building complete intelligent robotic systems. My work involves understanding how electronics, embedded systems, control algorithms, sensors, software architecture, and autonomous navigation come together to create machines that operate in the real world.

I hold an M.Tech in Robotics Engineering from M.S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences and a B.Tech in Mechatronics. I currently work as a Robotics Engineer at Kion Group. Before that, I led the hardware and software development of Curio, a museum guidance robot, at Vama Communications.

Engineering approach

Sense, perceive, decide, act.

I work at the boundaries between subsystems, where most robot problems actually live.

  1. 01

    Sense

    Choosing and integrating the sensors a robot depends on, and getting clean, timed data out of them.

  2. 02

    Perceive

    Turning raw measurements into an understanding of where the robot is and what surrounds it.

  3. 03

    Decide

    Planning paths and behaviours that are safe, efficient, and appropriate for the environment.

  4. 04

    Act

    Executing decisions through deterministic embedded control and reliable motor communication.
